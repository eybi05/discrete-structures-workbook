One algebra, three costumes

Lectures 1–5 study three systems: sets, propositions and logic circuits. Lecture 4–5 shows that they are one structure, a Boolean algebra. A law you learn in one column holds in the others, so this page keeps pointing back to the table below.

Duality

Each row is a law together with its dual: swap + with · and 0 with 1 (for sets, ∪ with ∩ and U with ∅; for logic, ∨ with ∧ and T with F). The principle of duality says the dual of a law is again a law. On a phone, the buttons above the table switch the notation.
