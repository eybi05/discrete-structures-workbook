Check anything

Checkers and tools

Use these to check your own work on any exercise, including ones from the notes. They understand the notation of both parts of the course: x′ or x' for complement, + and juxtaposition, or ¬ ∧ ∨ → ↔ ⊕.

Truth tables and equivalence

Type a proposition or a Boolean expression. You get its truth table with a column for each step, whether it is a tautology, and for up to 4 variables its CSoP, minimal sums and AND–OR circuit. Fill in the second box to test whether two expressions are equivalent.

Karnaugh map solver

Click squares to check them, or fill the map from an expression in x, y, z, t. The solver lists every prime implicant (filled chips are essential) and every minimal sum. Press “Random map” to practise; the answer stays hidden until you ask for it.

Venn diagram shader

Type a set expression in A, B, C (and U). The page shades its regions, names the fundamental products it contains, and gives the same set as a minimal Boolean expression.
